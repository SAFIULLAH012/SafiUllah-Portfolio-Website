Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Welcome to the portfolio website of Safi Ullah (safiulllah.pythonanywhere.com). Your privacy is important. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact form data: Name, email address, subject, and message — only when you voluntarily submit the contact form.
  • Analytics data: If you accept cookies, Google Analytics (GA4) collects anonymized data such as page views, browser type, and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is tracked.
  • No account data: This site has no user registration or login for visitors.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Contact form submissions are used solely to respond to your inquiry via email.
  • Analytics data is used to understand how visitors use the site and improve the content.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

This website uses two types of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Session and CSRF security cookies required for the contact form to function securely. These are always active and cannot be declined.
  • Analytics cookies (optional): Google Analytics GA4 cookies — only loaded if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. You may decline these at any time.

You can withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics: Anonymized usage data is sent to Google LLC. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Resend (Email): Contact form emails are sent via Resend. See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • WhatsApp: The WhatsApp Chat button links to WhatsApp's service. See WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

This site uses HTTPS encryption, CSRF protection on all forms, rate limiting, and secure headers. Contact form data is transmitted securely via email and is not stored in a public database.
